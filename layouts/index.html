A connected research programme

Four questions shape our work

Our work is language-first, question-led, and empirically demanding. The themes overlap by design: linguistic knowledge helps us understand reliability; structured knowledge supports factuality; security must work across languages and communities.

01

How do language and meaning shape AI?

We study multilinguality, linguistic typology, semantics, variation, and the representations learned by language models.

Language, meaning & multilinguality →
02

When can language technology be trusted?

We investigate memorisation, inversion, poisoning, privacy, robust evaluation, and responsible security practice.

Security, privacy & responsible AI →
03

How can models stay connected to evidence?

We combine knowledge graphs, factuality, fact-checking, attribution, and structured reasoning to make information more reliable.

Knowledge, factuality & reliability →
04

Who benefits from language technology?

We work across language communities and domains, including education, cultural heritage, science, finance, and low-resource settings.

Communities, domains & sustainability →

How we work

Setting people up for success

Excellent research depends on individual judgement and a healthy collective system: expertise should be visible, feedback routine, expectations clear, contributions recognised, and collaboration easier.

Our research culture
  1. RigourQuestions and evidence before venue or fashion.
  2. FeedbackShare work while it can still change.
  3. ResponsibilityIndependence without isolation.
  4. TrustOpen disagreement with respect and support.
