A shared intellectual framework

Four dimensions of trustworthy language technology

Our faculty pursue distinct programmes across four dimensions: linguistic validity; knowledge representation and grounding; integrity under failure or attack; and real-world fit and consequences for communities, institutions, and the material environment.

01 · Linguistic validity

Language, meaning & variation

We study linguistic structure, semantics, multilinguality, low-resource languages, and what model representations reveal about transfer and failure.

Explore language & representation →
02 · Representation & grounding

Knowledge, evidence & reasoning

We represent, complete, and reason over knowledge graphs, and connect model outputs to source documents, citations, and other traceable evidence.

Explore knowledge & grounding →
03 · System integrity

Security, reliability & risk

We study attacks, privacy, uncertainty, robustness, evaluation, and the conditions under which apparently capable systems break.

Explore reliability & risk →
04 · Real-world fit

Communities, domains & consequences

We examine how community priorities, values, institutional settings, and environmental and social costs change what responsible success means.

Explore communities & consequences →
