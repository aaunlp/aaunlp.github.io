Scope

What is included here?

We include the peer-reviewed 2024–26 work identified in a faculty-wide audit, together with selected earlier landmarks that make each research trajectory legible. Canonical links point to ACL Anthology, official proceedings, or publisher records.

Preprints are omitted when no peer-reviewed version exists, and duplicate arXiv versions are suppressed. The crawl was last reconciled in July 2026; accuracy takes priority over a superficially exhaustive list.
