Johannes leads AAU NLP and is Head of the Copenhagen Section of Aalborg University's Department of Computer Science. His research uses linguistic typology and formal semantics to study model representations, cross-lingual transfer and failure.

He leads projects on multilingual model security and the detection of tampered language models, and co-leads AI:SECURITY with Qiongxiu Li.
