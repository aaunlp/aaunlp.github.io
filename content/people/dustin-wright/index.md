His work tests whether generated claims are supported by source documents and how scientific information changes through summaries and reports. He also studies the diversity of claims and opinions expressed by language models, and whether evaluation metrics remain reliable when the data changes.

Dustin joined AAU NLP in February 2026, following a Danish Data Science Academy postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Copenhagen. His sustainability research combines technical work on efficient models with analysis of AI's wider environmental consequences. He co-supervises Luc Raszewski's DARA fellowship on diversity-preserving alignment with Johannes Bjerva.
