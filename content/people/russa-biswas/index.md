She develops methods for knowledge-graph completion and entity typing, combining graph structure with textual descriptions. Her recent work examines multilingual hallucinations and reasoning supported by explicit graph paths.

Russa co-directs AI:PAGE-Lab with historian Johan Heinsen. The lab turns historical texts into searchable, connected knowledge so that researchers can explore relationships across cultural-heritage collections.
